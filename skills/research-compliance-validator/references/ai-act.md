# EU AI Act Checklist for Research Projects

Reference: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act)

Note: The AI Act applies to AI *systems* placed on the EU market or affecting EU persons. Research/development projects may be partially exempt (Art. 2(6)) but best practice is to comply where possible. Mark N/A with justification if purely internal research not deployed.

## Risk Classification (Art. 6, 7, Annex III)
- [ ] **AI system risk tier identified**: The project has assessed whether any AI system falls into Prohibited, High-Risk, Limited-Risk, or Minimal-Risk categories
- [ ] **Prohibited practices avoided**: No use of subliminal manipulation, social scoring, real-time biometric surveillance in public spaces, or exploitation of vulnerabilities
- [ ] **High-risk determination documented**: If the system falls under Annex III (e.g., biometric ID, education, employment, access to services, law enforcement, critical infrastructure), this is acknowledged with compliance steps planned

## Data Governance (Art. 10)
- [ ] **Training data documented**: Sources, collection methods, and preprocessing of training/validation data are described
- [ ] **Data quality assessed**: Training data is checked for errors, completeness, and representativeness
- [ ] **Bias examination performed**: Training data has been examined for potential biases; mitigation measures documented
- [ ] **Sensitive attributes handled**: Special category data used in training is identified and handled appropriately

## Technical Documentation (Art. 11)
- [ ] **System documentation maintained**: Technical documentation describes the AI system's purpose, design, architecture, and intended use
- [ ] **Performance metrics recorded**: Accuracy, robustness, and other relevant metrics are documented
- [ ] **Known limitations documented**: The system's limitations and failure modes are explicitly described

## Transparency & Explainability (Art. 13, 50)
- [ ] **Outputs are interpretable**: The system provides explanations or confidence scores where appropriate
- [ ] **AI-generated content labelled**: Content generated by AI is identifiable as such (watermarking, disclosure)
- [ ] **Capability boundaries communicated**: Users/participants are informed about what the system can and cannot do

## Human Oversight (Art. 14)
- [ ] **Human oversight mechanism defined**: A human can review, override, or halt the AI system's decisions
- [ ] **Automated decisions reviewed**: High-stakes automated decisions have a human review step
- [ ] **Stop/override controls exist**: Mechanisms to disable or correct the system in real time are available

## Accuracy, Robustness & Cybersecurity (Art. 15)
- [ ] **Accuracy validated**: The system has been tested on a representative validation set
- [ ] **Robustness to adversarial inputs tested**: The system has been tested against out-of-distribution or adversarial inputs
- [ ] **Security measures implemented**: The system is protected against adversarial attacks and data poisoning

## Conformity & Registration (Art. 43, 49) [High-risk only]
- [ ] **Conformity assessment completed**: For high-risk systems, a conformity assessment has been conducted before deployment
- [ ] **EU database registration**: High-risk AI systems intended for deployment are registered in the EU AI Act database

## Scoring guidance
- Pass: Documented evidence in project files
- Partial: Partially addressed
- Fail: Not addressed despite being applicable
- N/A: Genuinely not applicable (e.g., no AI system developed, purely exempt research context)
